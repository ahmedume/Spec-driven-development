 Another thing to add is an architecture review phase before coding.

Instead of only asking: explain why you used this.

Also ask:

* What alternatives did you reject?
* What are the trade-offs?
* What breaks first under 10,000 users?
* What’s the weakest part of this architecture?
* What technical debt did we introduce?
* If this were an interview, how would I justify every major decision?
