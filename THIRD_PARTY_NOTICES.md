## F. Suggested hygiene

- Keep `LICENSE.txt`, `*-OFL.txt`, and this file unchanged and together — removing them breaks license compliance even when nothing else changes.
- When updating a skill, note the source URL in your commit message.
- If a skill's `SKILL.md` frontmatter says `author:`, mirror that in this file.
- Treat section B and C skills as inputs to be absorbed into your own work, not as things to redistribute.
